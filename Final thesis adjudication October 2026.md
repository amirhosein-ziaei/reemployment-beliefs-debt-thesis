The downloaded [Counterpoint March 2025 statements](https://counterpointfunds.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Counterpoint-3.31.25-Semi-Annual-Financial-Statements.pdf) report **$65,408** of recapture for Tactical Equity over six months and no remaining eligible balance for that fund at period end. #### Data specification and access

Use SEC EDGAR N-CSR/N-CSRS financial statements, N-1A/485 prospectuses and amendments, 497 supplements, and the corresponding adviser-hosted financial reports. Start with 2019–25 histories; extend toward the proposal's 2016 start only after measurement works. An accession identifies a document, a registrant CIK identifies the trust, and series/class identifiers identify the fund and share class. **CIK alone is not a fund identifier.** Adviser identities must be consolidated across trusts and name changes.

The indispensable observations are manually extracted fields, not a ready-made SEC numeric table: recovery amount in the reporting interval; opening and closing eligible claim balances by waiver/expiry vintage; new recoverable waivers; expired amounts; forgiven amounts; historical and current caps and exclusions; contract changes; average net assets; expense components; fiscal dates; and adviser/series/class links. Separate statement-period recoveries from “subject to possible recoupment” balances. Preserve a page/section citation for every extraction.

Annual and semiannual reports give interval timing; they do not create monthly transactions. Fiscal calendars differ, amendments arrive irregularly, and recent shareholder-report formatting changes mean that a short shareholder summary is not necessarily the financial-statement source. Historical EDGAR filings can be cached, but a reliable bulk route has **not** been demonstrated here: direct SEC downloads returned 403 while web reading and several adviser-hosted PDFs worked. Academic subscription access is unnecessary for the proposed raw reports, but public status does not settle practical Iran access. Test retrieval and repeat downloads on the student's normal connection; store a dated manifest and source hashes. Publish extraction code, citations and derived fields subject to source terms, rather than assuming all provider PDFs can be redistributed.

**Observed sample:** a handful of real fund reports, not a constructed panel. **Planning target, not an estimate:** roughly 80–150 funds across at least 25–30 economically independent advisers, with usable histories of several years. A smaller carefully bounded descriptive sample can support a thesis, but cannot automatically support market-wide claims or precise adviser-clustered inference.

#### Estimand and identification

The primary unit is fund/class × reporting interval, with claim vintages nested inside it when allocation is observable. First estimate the actual recovery charge in basis points of average net assets and its contribution to the difference between the fee borne by investors and the fee they would have borne **holding other accounting items fixed without that recovery**. This is an accounting counterfactual, not an equilibrium estimate of what the adviser would charge under a different contract.

The timing estimand is the difference in the fraction of eligible claims recovered when a predetermined claim vintage is in its final eligible interval versus earlier intervals, conditional on legal cap constraints and the risk set. If a report gives only aggregate recovery, do not assign it to the oldest vintage by assumption. Bound allocations across eligible vintages or exclude the interval from the vintage analysis.

**Identification: MECHANISM-CONSISTENT / ASSOCIATIONAL ONLY with the presently verified data.** Expiry is predetermined conditional on the original waiver, but waiver initiation, fund age, performance, asset growth, forgiveness and continuation are selected. A common three-year term also creates an age–period–cohort problem. Calendar effects plus fund effects do not magically isolate the deadline incentive. Realized headroom can be a mediator of growth and fee policy, not an exogenous control.

The smallest useful model relates interval recovery/eligible opening balance to a final-interval indicator and a prespecified measure of available cap room, with fund and calendar effects where support permits. It is diagnostic. Compare actual expiry schedules with placebo shifted dates; show pre-expiry profiles; distinguish exact from interval-censored expiration; leave out each adviser; and retain funds that close, merge, change cap or forgive claims. Cluster at adviser and consider calendar dependence; hundreds of share classes do not equal hundreds of independent decisions. With few advisers, emphasize adviser-level summaries and uncertainty, not conventional clustered significance.

A causal expiry claim requires variation in contractual deadlines or other genuinely defensible differences that are not just fund age or waiver cohort. If no such variation exists, preserve the descriptive finance question and remove causal language. Do not spend months manufacturing an instrument.

#### Economic value and null survival

The audience is delegated asset management, fee contracting and investor protection. The useful general lesson would be how an earlier subsidy changes who benefits when operating costs fall. Precision in basis points and aggregate dollars matters more than the sophistication of the text extractor. Recovery is also compensation for an earlier subsidy; its presence alone does not establish exploitation or a welfare loss.

**Null classification: NULL STILL PRODUCES A MEANINGFUL PAPER only if the ledger is broad and precise enough to bound economically important recovery or deadline concentration.** No acceleration near expiry can be informative if recoveries, forgiveness and expired rights are reliably measured. A sparse collection of zero recaptures merely leaves a useful institutional thesis with weak publication value. Low power is not an economic null.

**Publication category: FIELD-JOURNAL POTENTIAL IF KEY GATE PASSES; otherwise GOOD MASTER'S THESIS ONLY.** Financial Management, Journal of Banking & Finance and related asset-management outlets are plausible families for excellent execution, not forecasts of acceptance. The 2026 affiliated-fund paper above has a large coherent sample and an established investor-outcome mechanism. This project needs a validated ledger, material incidence, transparent sample selection and a timing test beyond arithmetic. A causal claim is optional; defensible incremental measurement is not.

**Execution risk: HIGH before pilot, MODERATE only if histories reconcile.** After the pilot, budget roughly 3 weeks for institutions/literature, 6–8 for extraction and linking, 3–4 for double-coding and reconciliation, 2 for descriptive results, 4 for identification and sensitivity, and 5 for writing/revision; overlap faculty feedback throughout and reserve 2 weeks for failed links or report changes. The likely month-consuming task is resolving inconsistent footnotes across successive reports, not computation. Cap the population and stop adding fund types.

**Referee objection 1:** “Your expiry pattern is just the age of waivers and successful funds.” **ANSWERABLE ONLY IF PILOT GATE PASSES** for a causal interpretation; otherwise answer by making the primary result accounting incidence and labeling the timing evidence associative.

**Referee objection 2:** “Your ledger mistakes a shrinking eligible balance for a payment to the adviser.” **ANSWERABLE ONLY IF PILOT GATE PASSES.** Reconcile opening claims + new claims − recovered − expired − forgiven = closing claims, allowing disclosed adjustments. A language model's extraction confidence cannot replace this identity.

#### Estimand and design

Primary contemporaneous estimand: the change in reported debt-payment probability associated with a **10 percentage-point decrease in conditional finding probability**, holding reported job-loss probability fixed, and how that slope differs across job-loss beliefs. The unit is eligible employed person-month. A simple prespecified regression uses person and month effects, loss probability, finding difficulty and their centered interaction; state-by-time effects are a robustness choice only where cells support them.

**Identification: MECHANISM-CONSISTENT / ASSOCIATIONAL ONLY.** No random exposure or defensible instrument has been established. Person effects absorb stable pessimism but not changes in pessimism, deteriorating finances, anticipated separation, changing debts or survey response style. Controlling many other beliefs can remove part of the actual mechanism or add noise; compare a small prespecified baseline and an expanded sentiment specification instead of searching controls until one result survives.

A separate prediction exercise asks whether these beliefs improve forecasts of **next month's reported debt-payment expectation**, conditional on its current value and available predictors. Use held-out later calendar periods and entirely held-out people; overlapping rotating panels must not leak the same person's responses across train and test. This is prediction of subjective distress, not prediction of actual delinquency. Avoid an unqualified short-panel fixed-effects regression with a lagged outcome; its bias is not solved by adding more rows.

Audit endpoint heaping at 0/50/100, within-person variation, survey tenure, attrition and employment transitions. Compare a baseline-employed cohort with the changing employed sample to expose selection. Show contemporaneous and lagged results separately, placebo future-belief associations, and sensitivity to common financial-sentiment controls. Report person-clustered uncertainty and calendar-block sensitivity; a national episode is not thousands of independent shocks. Bootstrap generated sentiment summaries and the full prediction comparison where used. Prespecify one main interaction and one held-out prediction comparison rather than a long heterogeneity menu.

#### Value, feasibility and referee test

The finance audience is household debt, income risk and consumption smoothing. The question is finance-legible because debt commitments respond differently to a short interruption and prolonged inability to regain acceptable work. The data do not measure a duration distribution, however, so “reemployment difficulty” is a safer construct than “expected unemployment duration.” Its admissions value comes from careful survey interpretation, panel analysis, magnitudes and honest inference. It should not become an ML forecasting competition.

**Null classification: NULL LEAVES A USEFUL THESIS BUT WEAK PUBLICATION CASE.** A precise small incremental role can discipline the use of job-loss beliefs as a summary of household risk. It is less likely to support a strong field paper because neither a new causal mechanism nor realized financial behavior is observed.

**Publication category: GOOD MASTER'S THESIS ONLY at present; CREDIBLE WORKING PAPER if the added signal is precisely measured, stable and financially substantive.** Household-finance work in JBF or financial-economics outlets is the relevant family; the nearby Labour Economics paper provides a more demanding benchmark through administrative linkage. Do not infer field-journal potential from the mere availability of a large SCE panel. A credible paper needs a finance-specific increment beyond Mitra's labor-risk interaction and beyond generic pessimism.

**Execution risk: LOW to MODERATE.** After a 2-week pilot, allow 3 weeks for literature/routing, 3 for harmonization, 3 for measurement/attrition, 4 for primary and prediction analyses, 3 for sensitivity, 5 for writing and feedback, leaving substantial buffer. The main source of unexpected delay is attempting to attach wealth or debt modules that sharply shrink the panel. The core thesis must work without them.

**Referee objection 1:** “You have replaced job search in a recent SCE paper with another pessimistic answer.” **ANSWERABLE ONLY IF PILOT GATE PASSES** through financial interpretation, within-person evidence, persistence and incremental predictive content; a cross-sectional significant coefficient is not an answer.

**Referee objection 2:** “You infer actual debt risk and unemployment duration from incompatible subjective questions.” **REFEREE OBJECTION ANSWERABLE** by using the actual constructs, reporting the horizons and narrowing all conclusions. The stronger causal/default claim would be probably fatal without additional data.
